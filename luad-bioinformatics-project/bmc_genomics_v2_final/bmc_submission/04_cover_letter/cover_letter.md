Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript, "A literature-guided public-data evidence hierarchy prioritises a PLK1-centred mitotic/redox programme in lung adenocarcinoma," for consideration as a Research article in BMC Genomics.

Public lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) transcriptomic studies frequently combine discovery, validation, biological context and functional screens without separating their inferential strength, which invites overinterpretation. We address this by constructing a transparent, literature-guided public-data evidence hierarchy and applying it to a PLK1-centred mitotic/redox programme, stating explicitly what each layer can and cannot support.

Our main findings are: (i) a compact nine-gene programme score with moderate held-out TCGA discrimination (C-index 0.640) that transfers significantly in four of five independent GEO cohorts, with GSE37745 retained as a transparent external non-replication; (ii) coherent pathway, Human Protein Atlas and Visium tissue-context support; and (iii) prioritisation of PLK1 as an experimental dependency node, together with explicit negative boundaries — PLK1 is not LUAD-selective, ERO1A is not a PLK-inhibitor stratifier, and no normal-lung therapeutic window is demonstrated.

The study is a transparent, fully reproducible transcriptomic public-data analysis that fits the scope of BMC Genomics.
